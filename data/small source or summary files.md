## Small Source of Summary Files

| **File**               | **Format**                  | **Purpose**                                                                             | **Source**                       |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Genome sequence**    | FASTA (.fasta)              | Used to upload the complete *Cocos nucifera* chloroplast genome to Galaxy for analysis. | NCBI RefSeq, **NC_022417.1**     |
| **Annotated genome**   | GenBank (.gb / .gbk)        | Used to identify genes and other features found in the chloroplast genome.              | NCBI RefSeq, **NC_022417.1**     |
| **Genome summary**     | TXT / Markdown (.txt / .md) | Provides a summary of the organism, genome size, GC content, and genome structure.      | **NCBI RefSeq + Galaxy**         |
| **Source information** | TXT / Markdown (.txt / .md) | Keeps track of the database and accession number used in the analysis.                  | **NCBI Nucleotide, NC_022417.1** |
